## What the last two months cost

The **$281 spent since Jun 14** is almost entirely unplanned rework:

- **Leak chase:** ~$100 (Flex Seal, MarineWeld, extra hardener, leak alarm)
- **Hatch insert rework:** ~$36 (hex inserts, hex bits, penetrant, extractor)
- **Tools bought mid-problem:** ~$110 (rotary tool, carbide burrs, 25/64" bit)
- **Actually-planned parts:** ~$20 (drive pin, webbing, hinge screws, clear coat)

For V2 budgeting: the tools carry forward, but the leak-chase spend was avoidable — sealing laminate cut edges with neat epoxy during layup costs almost nothing.
